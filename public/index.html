    How to read

    • QM (Quasimodo): price makes a higher high, then breaks the previous low. Enter when price retests the left shoulder level.
    • H&S: three peaks with the head highest. Confirmed when the neckline breaks.
    • Double top / bottom: two peaks or troughs at the same level.
    • Status Forming means not yet confirmed. Wait for confirmation before entering.
